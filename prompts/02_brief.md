You are writing a content brief that a freelance writer or a drafting agent can execute
without a call. The editor has already picked the angle.

Inputs: the chosen angle, the SME transcript, the voice spec, and (optionally) the AI-search
tracker showing which buyer questions we are not cited for.

The brief must contain:

1. **Headline + one-sentence thesis**
2. **Reader**: role, company size, the moment they search for this
3. **Primary buyer question** this piece should be the cited answer to in ChatGPT, Perplexity and
   Google AI answers, plus 3–5 follow-up questions it must answer in plain sentences
4. **Outline**: H2s as claims, not topics. Under each, the specific point and the evidence for it
5. **Proof inventory**: every quote, number and example the draft may use, each tagged
   `[Observed]`, `[Estimated]`, `[Benchmarked]` or `[Assumed]` with its source. The draft may not
   introduce a number that isn't on this list.
6. **What we are NOT saying**: claims to avoid (legal, competitive, overpromising)
7. **Answer-engine structure**: a 40–60 word direct answer block near the top, one comparison
   table or list a model can lift, named entities spelled consistently
8. **Distribution plan**: the 3 places this gets repurposed and who posts each
9. **Success metric** for this specific piece, and the date we check it

HUMAN CHECKPOINT: the SME approves the proof inventory before drafting starts.
